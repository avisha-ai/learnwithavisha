## Format proposal — "Plant Mysteries" (Claude, 14 Oct)
60–90 s per video:
1. **Hook (first 3 s):** a real plant puzzle. "Same exchanger, same fluids — why does flipping one flow make it work better?"
2. **Principle:** the clean animated diagram, one idea, big burned-in captions (most people watch muted).
3. **Payoff:** the answer, said simply.
4. **Pull to come back:** "Next: why every exchanger slowly gets worse — and nobody notices."
- Something changes on screen every few seconds; one idea per video; end with the series line.
- Each video → a YouTube Short + the Thursday LinkedIn "ChE in 60 seconds" post + an answer on the Ask site.
