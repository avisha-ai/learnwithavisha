## Engineering truth

Fouling reduces the overall heat transfer coefficient U silently over time.
The layer builds on the tube wall, resistance rises, and the hot side outlet
temperature climbs. Pressure drop across the exchanger stays nearly flat,
because the flow path is not blocked — only insulated. That is why no alarm
triggers, and why the instrument that would catch it earliest is the one
nobody watches closely enough.
